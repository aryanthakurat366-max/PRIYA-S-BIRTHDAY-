You're one of those people who makes things better just by being around — the random calls, the inside jokes, the way you always show up when it matters. Not everyone gets a friend like that, and I don't take it for granted.

Over this past year, I've seen you handle things with a strength that honestly amazes me, and I've laughed harder with you than with almost anyone else. You deserve a year that matches your energy. May it bring you clarity where you've felt stuck, confidence in the choices you make, and plenty of moments worth remembering. Keep being unapologetically you — it's rare and it's worth celebrating.

25 is a proper milestone, Priya — quarter of a century, and honestly you're handling it better than most people twice your age. Here's to another year of you being exactly who you are. Hope today is full of the things that make you happiest.
